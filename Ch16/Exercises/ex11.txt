struct {
    double a;
    union {
        char b[4];
        double c;
        int d;
    } e;
    char f[4];
}

i) total size of variable e is 8 bytes
ii) size of f is 4 bytes
iii) compiler align the member of structure by leaving holes after the space of f is obtained.
iv) max size of member of s is 8 bytes
v) so the f's offset must be a multiple of 8 bytes so f will get a 4bytes of hole

8bytes(double a) + 8bytes(union e) + 8bytes(f and holes) = 24bytes

In concluison, total size of s is "24bytes"
